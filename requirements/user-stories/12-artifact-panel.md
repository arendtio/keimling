# User stories: Artifact panel

## US-43 — Open the panel with nothing loaded

**As** a user, **I want** an empty artifact panel to match the light or dark background, **so that** I do not see a blank white page.

**Acceptance:** traces REQ-AR-1.

## US-44 — Switch artifacts from the panel

**As** a user, **I want** the workspace’s artifacts listed at the top of the open panel, newest first, **so that** I can switch without scrolling past the preview.

**Acceptance:** traces REQ-AR-2. Choosing another row shows that artifact. Choosing the previous row brings it back.
